### Maker first comment (draft)
Hi everyone — I'm Wade (MastaTriLL). I built Emvori because most AI tools either invent citations or take actions before you notice.

Emvori is private by default. Anonymous chat keeps history in your browser. Research only answers from fetched public pages and refuses when the evidence is thin. Agents can plan and draft, but Gmail is draft-only and external Operator steps do not run until you approve them. Unapproved steps are treated as a product defect.

It does not train its own public foundation model; it routes through managed providers with fallback. Plans are transparent (Free / Pro $20 / Enterprise request-only) with hard daily allowances that reset UTC.

The public status and trust surface is already live: https://mastatrill.github.io/emvori-status/

I'd love feedback on the approval gates and the honest-research behavior. What would make this useful for your research or agent workflows?
